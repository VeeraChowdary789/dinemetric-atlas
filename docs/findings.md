# Findings from the supplied snapshot

The India sample contains 8,652 listings across 43 cities. Only 6,513 are rated, yielding an average of 3.352 out of 5. The remaining 2,139 India listings (24.7%) are unrated and excluded from that average.

## Supply is geographically concentrated

New Delhi has 5,473 listings, Gurgaon 1,118 and Noida 1,080. Together they account for 88.7% of the India sample. Treat this as a Delhi NCR-heavy listing dataset rather than a representative national restaurant census.

## Common cuisines overlap

North Indian appears in 3,946 India listings, Chinese in 2,688 and Fast Food in 1,963. A restaurant can serve multiple cuisines, so these counts should not be summed to estimate the total number of restaurants.

## Pricing and services

The median positive listed cost for two in India is ₹450. Online delivery is listed at 2,423 restaurants (28.0%), and table booking at 1,111 (12.8%). These flags describe availability at the snapshot, not usage or the present day.

## Questions for further research

- Is coverage comparable across cities and price segments?
- Does the rating–price association remain after controlling for city, cuisine and vote volume?
- How recently were delivery and booking flags updated?
- Can transaction or footfall data validate demand before making an investment decision?

These are research questions, not findings established by this dataset.
